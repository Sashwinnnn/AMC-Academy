04 // Core Tactical Theory

Strategic Guessing Elimination

The Elimination Engine:

1. Rule Out Outliers: If 4 choices are fractions with denominator 7 and one is denominator 12, inspect the outlier critically.

2. Test Extreme Values: Substitute extreme cases (n=1, x=0) to eliminate invalid generic choices.

3. Work Backwards: Test answer choice (C) first; if too small or large, eliminate half the options immediately.

06 // Live Demonstration

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Question 25 Simulation: A speed train travels at 60 mph for the first half of a distance and 30 mph for the second half. What is the average speed?

1. Avoid the Trap:
    The arithmetic average 60+302=45 is a TRAP choice designed to catch quick test-takers.

2. Set Distance Smartly:
    Let each half distance equal 60 miles. First half time = 1 hr. Second half time = 2 hrs.

3. Harmonic Average Formula:
    Avg Speed=Total DistanceTotal Time=1201+2=40mph.
