04 // Core Tactical Theory

The Area Ratio Blueprint

Geometric Formula: P(Target)=Area of Favorable RegionTotal Area of Region

1. Calculate Total Region Area: Find the area of the entire bounded space (e.g., outer square or circle).

2. Isolate Favorable Region Area: Calculate the exact area of the winning target zone.

3. Simplify the Ratio: Divide favorable area by total area, canceling common variables like π or side length terms.

05 // Advanced Theory

Independent Events & Complementary Chance

P(At Least One) = 1 − P(None Happen)

INDEPENDENT MULTIPLICATION: For independent events A and B, the chance of both occurring is P(A)×P(B).
COMPLEMENTARY SHORTCUT: Finding the probability that an event *does not* happen and subtracting from 1 saves massive calculation time.

06 // Live Demonstration

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Question: Two fair 6-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the product of the numbers is EVEN?

1. Identify Total Outcomes:
    Rolling two 6-sided dice gives 6×6=36 total equally likely outcomes.

2. Use Complementary Probability:
    A product is ODD only if BOTH dice land on odd numbers.
    P(Die 1 is Odd)=12, P(Die 2 is Odd)=12 → P(Product Odd)=12×12=14.

3. Calculate Even Product Probability:
    P(Product Even)=1−14=34
